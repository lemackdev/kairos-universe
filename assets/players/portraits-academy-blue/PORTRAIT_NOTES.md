# Academy Blue Portraits

Five fictional, AI-generated player portraits use the approved [2027 Academy Blue uniform](../../../data/uniforms/kairos-academy-blue-2027.json). The [manifest](manifest.json) records their source records, ages at the 2026-11-24 narrative date, dimensions, and hashes.

The faces and physical traits are visual concepts, not additions to character canon. The backgrounds draw on confirmed hometowns but do not document where or when a player was photographed. The uniform is an approved design, not proof of a current sponsorship contract.

The final selection was compared with the ten existing main-roster portraits. Mako, xNox, and Murmur were regenerated after the first drafts overlapped visually with existing players. Kite and Grit kept their original faces; only their backgrounds were revised.
