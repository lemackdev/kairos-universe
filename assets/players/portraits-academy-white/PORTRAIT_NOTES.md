# Academy White Portraits

Five fictional, AI-generated player portraits use the approved [2027 Academy White uniform](../../../data/uniforms/kairos-academy-white-2027.json). The [manifest](manifest.json) records the source player records, ages at the 2026-11-24 narrative date, dimensions, and hashes.

The faces and physical traits are visual concepts, not additions to character canon. Hometown-inspired backgrounds do not document where or when a player was photographed. The uniform is an approved design, not proof of a current sponsorship contract.

The selection was compared visually with the existing Main and Academy Blue portraits. Hair length, face shape, build, expression, and skin texture vary across the five, rather than using one idealized face.
