# Counter-Strike Academy Portraits

Five fictional, AI-generated player portraits use the approved [2027 CS Academy Green uniform](../../../data/uniforms/kairos-cs-academy-green-2027.json). The [manifest](manifest.json) records the source player records, ages at the 2026-11-24 narrative date, dimensions, and hashes.

The faces and physical traits are visual concepts, not additions to character canon. Backgrounds evoke confirmed current cities, not documented portrait locations or dates. The uniform is an approved design, not proof of a current sponsorship contract.

The selection was compared visually with the existing Main, Academy Blue, and Academy White portraits. Nolan and Reid Hart have plausible sibling resemblance because they are confirmed twins, but are not depicted as identical; canon does not establish whether they are identical twins.
