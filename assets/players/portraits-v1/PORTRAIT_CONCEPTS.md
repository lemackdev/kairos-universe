## Art Direction

The goal is a consistent media-day session with five distinct, age-appropriate
people: different facial structures, hair, proportions, skin details and
expressions. Natural texture and restrained lighting replace glossy beauty
retouching. Hometown-inspired backgrounds are subdued visual cues, not literal
archival photographs or evidence that a story event occurred in that city.

## Canon Boundary

- All five nationalities are currently TBD. No country, ethnicity or ancestry
  was inferred from a city or surname.
- Physical traits are proposed artwork, not confirmed character facts.
- Current canon lists no accepted sponsors. There are no sponsor marks.
- The source mentions a gold Kairos helmet and gold logo, but the logo folder
  contains no approved artwork to reproduce.
- Black uniforms with gold details appear in the Counter-Strike source. The
  League jerseys here are design concepts, not a new canonical uniform record.
- Plain KAIROS lettering is a placeholder, not a replacement organization logo.
- No biographies, original episodes, organization records or website publication
  settings were changed for this collection.

## Approval and Future Revisions

Approve or request changes by nickname. Preserve the player ID mapping and the
original files. Any revised render should use a new versioned filename, retain
its prompt and keep image provenance explicit. Approval of artwork must not
silently add ethnicity, nationality or other unsupported biography to canon.

After approval, the website can use derivative optimized images while these
original PNGs remain the source assets. No portrait is enabled automatically.
