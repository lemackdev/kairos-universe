# Main Roster Portraits, Version 2

Ten AI-generated fictional portraits in the approved 2027 black Kairos kit.
The five League images revise the original identities; the five CS images are
new visual proposals. All are 1024 x 1536 PNGs, copied without alteration from
the image generator. The [manifest](manifest.json) records identities, hashes,
age context, source records and visual direction.

These are illustrative portraits, not documentary photographs or new biography.
Acne, braces, facial hair and expressions vary intentionally. No ethnicity,
medical history, contract, sponsorship announcement or story event is implied.
FLACOGOMEZ is 17 at the narrative date; the other four CS players are 18.
His background is generic California, not an invented hometown or Brazilian visit.

The original League portraits remain under portraits-v1. Their older nationality
and uniform notes are historical production context, not current canon.
Publication is controlled by the existing website manifest and generated at build
time. Pages label the artwork as AI-generated.
