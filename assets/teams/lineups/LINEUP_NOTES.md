# Kairos Team Lineup Artwork

Five original, panoramic lineup compositions use the [approved player portraits](../../players/) as identity and uniform references. The [manifest](manifest.json) records the left-to-right player IDs and validates the image files.

The roster names, roles, and captain labels on Kairospedia are rendered from current structured canon, not embedded in the artwork. The poses and studio settings are visual concepts, not a documented photoshoot or narrative event. The approved 2027 uniforms do not establish a current sponsorship contract.

These assets cover the five published Kairos teams with complete approved portrait sets. They do not represent external real-world teams, whose player photographs have not been approved for this project.
