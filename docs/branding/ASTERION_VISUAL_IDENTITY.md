# Asterion Visual Identity

Asterion Intelligence is Kairos Esports' **Parent Company** and **Principal Institutional Partner**, not an unrelated external sponsor. Its silver orbital-A and star identity represents artificial intelligence, technology, research, infrastructure, capital, long-term investment and the institutional structure supporting Kairos. These are approved brand meanings, not new claims about particular corporate products or financial figures.

The [standalone logo](../../assets/branding/asterion/asterion-intelligence-logo.png) is the original supplied PNG, unchanged at 877 x 887 pixels. Keep it independently reusable and preserve its aspect ratio.

Asterion occupies the principal front-partner position on approved jerseys. This does not replace Kairos as the sporting identity. See [placements](PARTNER_PLACEMENT.md) and the [organization record](../../data/organizations/asterion-intelligence.json).
