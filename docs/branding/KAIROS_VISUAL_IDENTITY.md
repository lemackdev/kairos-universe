# Kairos Visual Identity

Kairos Esports is the primary sporting identity. Its approved gold warrior/helmet emblem expresses discipline, timing, preparation, courage, excellence and competitive ambition. Kairos means the decisive or opportune moment. Gold expresses the organization's highest competitive standard.

The [standalone logo](../../assets/branding/kairos/kairos-esports-logo.png) is preserved byte-for-byte from the supplied PNG. It remains separate from the Asterion mark and must not be recreated from a jersey crop. The original logo is 877 x 887 pixels; no artificial upscaling was applied.

Use restrained identity presentation, not sponsor walls or commerce. Preserve aspect ratio and clear space. Approved colors belong to the [2027 uniform family](2027_UNIFORM_SYSTEM.md). The black kit is shared by both main teams, not exclusive to League.
