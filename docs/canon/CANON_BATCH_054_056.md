  Fletcher's care.

  far from him.

  campus medical resources.

  dimensions, or schedule is confirmed.

## Episode 55: A Escolha

**Exact date and time:** Saturday, November 7, 2026, 08:15

- All 143 eligible members of the Kairos community vote by secret ballot.
- Twelve unnamed restaurant chains are considered.
- The proposal permits optional food access and prohibits gambling, alcohol,
  performance metrics, competitive influence, and individual athlete
  endorsements.

## Episode 56: A Decisão

**Exact date and time:** Saturday, November 7, 2026, 19:30

- All 143 eligible voters participate.
- The result is 126 yes votes and 17 no votes.
- In-N-Out Burger, Chipotle Mexican Grill, and Chick-fil-A receive the highest
  preferences.
- Each person may use each restaurant once per week, with a maximum of three
  fast-food meals per week enforced through the campus badge.
- The result authorizes invitations and negotiations only.
- No partnership, contract, opening, construction, or restaurant response is
  confirmed.
- Aphromoo stands beside Thinkcard.
- 404 speaks with Moss.
- Vanta and Aster remain in their established Jungle and ADC roles.
