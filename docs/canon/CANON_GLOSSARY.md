# Canon Glossary

This glossary defines the terms used throughout Kairos Universe records.

## Canon

A fact confirmed by the narrative or an approved source-of-truth record. Canon
is treated as true in the current fictional universe unless it is later
retconned.

## Soft canon

A provisional detail supported by context but not formally confirmed. Soft
canon must be labeled and may be revised without creating a retcon.

## Unconfirmed

An idea, report, interpretation, or incomplete detail that lacks enough support
to be treated as canon.

## Retcon

An explicit removal, replacement, or reinterpretation of previously established
canon. Retcons must be recorded in
[RETCON_HISTORY.md](../retcons/RETCON_HISTORY.md).

## Non-canonical

Material that does not belong to the official continuity, such as an abandoned
draft, experiment, alternate scenario, or illustrative example.

## World state

The current canonical condition of organizations, teams, characters, and
ongoing objectives at a specific story point. The active snapshot is maintained
in [CURRENT_WORLD_STATE.md](../world-state/CURRENT_WORLD_STATE.md).

## Timeline

The ordered record of canonical events. Event order may be confirmed even when
exact dates are **TBD**.

## Episode record

A structured account of one narrative episode, including its stable identity,
story date when known, events, first appearances, and world-state changes.

## Character record

A structured source-of-truth profile for a person in the universe, identified
by a stable ID and slug.

## Organization record

A structured source-of-truth profile for a company or esports organization,
including its identity, relationships, status, and confirmed facts.

## Division

The branch of an esports organization responsible for one game, including its
teams, staff, and competitive operations.

## Main roster

The primary competitive lineup for a division. A planned or training roster is
not considered officially debuted until it plays a confirmed official match.

## Academy

A development team with a genuine training and progression purpose. Academy
status does not imply a specific competition unless that participation is
confirmed.

## First appearance

The earliest confirmed episode or canonical source in which a character,
organization, team, or other subject appears.

## Story date

The in-universe time at which an event or world-state snapshot occurs. It may
be exact, relative, or **TBD**.

## Real-world reference

A real person, organization, competition, place, or historical fact used as
background within the fictional universe. Real-world background does not make
fictional involvement real.

## Historical archive

An immutable source preserving what the original episodes said, including
later-retconned names, chronology drift, factual errors, and duplicate source
material. The archive is evidence of publication history, not the highest
priority for current canon.

## Living canon

The current repository state after approved corrections, retcons, and
chronology normalization. Living canon may evolve, but changes must not rewrite
the historical archive.
