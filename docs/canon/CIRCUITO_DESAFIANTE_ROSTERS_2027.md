The eight Circuito Desafiante 2027 teams are filled from the author's
Leaguepedia roster screenshots. Estral Esports and 7REX field only
Spanish-speaking players (Argentina, Chile and Peru). The other six teams field
Brazilian players. Bwipo (Belgium flag) is not eligible, and Envy, Brance,
Telas, Leleko, Netuno, Yupps, Grevthar, Namiru and Viciun already start in CBLOL
2027, so they are not used. The Peruvian Relax is left out because his nickname
collides with the Taiwanese Relax in LDL. Surplus players in the screenshots
were not created, and coaches are not part of the player rosters. Players were
reshuffled across the teams.
