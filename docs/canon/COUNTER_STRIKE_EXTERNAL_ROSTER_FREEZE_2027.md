## Rule

All external Counter-Strike 2 team rosters selected and approved by the author
for the initial 2027 competitive ecosystem remain unchanged through
**2027-05-23, inclusive**, the final day of the PGL Copenhagen Major 2027
(2027-05-07 through 2027-05-23), unless the author explicitly overrides this
directive.

- The earliest period in which the author may initiate external CS2 roster
  changes is **2027-05-24**. This is not an automatic transfer date; no
  transfer, retirement, promotion, benching, or contract expiration occurs
  automatically.
- The policy applies only to author-selected organizations and their approved
  rosters. The author has not yet supplied the external CS2 roster list, so none
  is established or imported.
- The freeze implies no tournament participation or qualification, for Kairos or
  any external team.
- Frozen real-world source snapshots are unchanged.
