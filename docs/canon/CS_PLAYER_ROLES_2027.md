# Counter-Strike 2 Player Roles, 2027

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED
**Source type:** Real-world reference (HLTV team pages, fetched 2026-11-09)

Backs `data/competitive-world-overrides/cs-player-roles-2027.json` (250 players).

Roles follow the role icons on each team's HLTV page:

- crosshair icon: **AWPer**
- crown icon: **IGL / Rifler** (in-game leader)
- both icons: **IGL / AWPer**
- no icon: **Rifler**, as directed by the author

Roles are matched by nickname to the team's 2027 lineup. A player whose nickname is
not on the HLTV page of their team (EYEBALLERS' dex) defaults to Rifler.
