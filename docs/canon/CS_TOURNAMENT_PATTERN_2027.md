# Counter-Strike Tournament Page Pattern 2027

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED (author directive)

Every Counter-Strike tournament except the two Majors follows the page pattern of IEM Dallas 2027.

| Tier | Teams | Group stage | Playoffs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 (premier, non-Major) | 16 | 4 groups of 4 | quarter-finals, semi-finals, grand final |
| Tier 2 | 16 | 4 groups of 4 | quarter-finals, semi-finals, grand final |
| Tier 3 | 8 | 2 groups of 4 | semi-finals, grand final |

- The two best teams of each group advance. Every match is a best of three, except the grand final (best of five).
- Each group plays a round robin of six matches in three rounds of two simultaneous matches.
- The page shows the group tables (M, RW, RL, RD, Record), the group-stage matches, the playoff bracket and the prize cards.
- Prize split (calculated from the prize pool, not announced): 16 teams 40% / 15% / 7.5% x2 / 4% x4 / 1.75% x8; 8 teams 45% / 20% / 10% x2 / 3.75% x4. Amounts are rounded to USD 5 and the winner absorbs the difference.
- Events whose teams are not known show `TBD` for every team slot. IEM Dallas 2027 and CCT North America #1 have invited teams and a random draw (see their invite documents). The group slots, the bracket and the prizes of the other events are filled in as teams are confirmed.
- The Majors (`pgl-copenhagen-major-2027`, `starladder-berlin-major-2027`) keep their format TBD.
- The 8-team structure (two groups of four, semi-finals and a grand final) is derived from the 16-team pattern; the author only stated the team count.

## Playoff seeding rule (16 teams, 4 groups of 4)

- The eight advancing teams are seeded 1 to 8 from the group-stage results: the four group winners take seeds 1-4 and the four runners-up seeds 5-8; inside each tier the order is wins, then round difference, then name.
- The quarter-finals are always 1-8, 2-7, 3-6 and 4-5. The bracket lists them as 1-8, 4-5, 2-7, 3-6 so that 1-8 meets 4-5 and 2-7 meets 3-6 in the semi-finals.
- Until every group match is played the slots show `Seed 1` to `Seed 8`; once the group stage is complete the teams are filled in automatically.
- The rule applies to the 16-team format only; the 8-team (Tier 3) format has no seeding rule yet.
