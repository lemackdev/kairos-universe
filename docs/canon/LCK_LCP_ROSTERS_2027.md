## Corrections to earlier canon

- **Loki** is not retired. His LCS retirement and its Cloud9 departure record were
  removed from `lcs-rosters-2027.json`. He starts Mid for BRION (Hanjin BRION).
  His Cloud9 history is preserved; Cloud9's 2027 lineup is unchanged.
- **Taeyoon** is not retired and no longer a free agent. FEARX history is kept and
  his free-agent record is stored as prior status on his Nongshim RedForce
  starting slot.
- **Keria** starts Support for KT Rolster; **Effort** starts Support for Hanwha Life Esports.
- **Diable** is retired exactly once.
- **Incoming players:** TheShy, BuLLDoG, Kael, kyeahoo and Poby left the Korean
  market list and are LCK starters; JunJia left the LCP market list and starts
  Jungle for SoftBank HAWKS gaming. Their market and "available" departure
  records in the LPL and LEC files were replaced by transfer departures here.
  Burdol and Viper stay unassigned.
