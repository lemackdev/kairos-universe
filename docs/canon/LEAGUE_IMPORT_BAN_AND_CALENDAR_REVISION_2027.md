# Author Directive: 2027 League Import Ban, Regional Eligibility, and Calendar Revision

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED
**Source type:** Author-confirmed canon directive, depicted in Episodes 111 (2027-01-26) and 113 (2027-01-27)

This document backs the Kairos Universe 2027 League of Legends regional player
eligibility ruleset
([`data/rulesets/league-regional-player-eligibility-2027.json`](../../data/rulesets/league-regional-player-eligibility-2027.json))
and the revised League Tier 1 Stage 1 calendar. It lives in the Kairos Universe
continuity layer. The frozen pre-Worlds 2026 reference dataset
(`external-data/pre-worlds-2026/`) is not modified.

## Regional eligibility (announced 2027-01-26)

Applies to the 2027 season, League of Legends Tier 1 and official Tier 2
regional competition.

| Region | Eligible territory | Import alias |
|---|---|---|
| LCS | United States, Canada, Mexico, Central America, Caribbean | NA |
| LEC | Eligible European, Middle Eastern, and African competitive territories | EU |
| LCK | South Korea | KR |
| LPL | Mainland China | CH |
| LCP | Asia-Pacific (Taiwan, Vietnam, Japan, Southeast Asia, Oceania), excluding mainland China and South Korea | PCS |
| CBLOL | Brazil and the rest of South America | SA |

The aliases are spreadsheet/import aliases, not official league names.

Rules: no imports; valid dual citizens select one eligible region per season and
the selection is locked; residency alone grants no eligibility; coaches,
analysts, and support staff are exempt; existing contracts are not
automatically voided; ineligible players cannot be registered for official
matches in the affected region; private international scrims remain permitted;
Counter-Strike is unaffected.

## Revised Tier 1 Stage 1 (announced 2027-01-27)

| | Previous | Revised |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 (LCS, LEC, LCK, LPL, LCP, CBLOL) | 2027-02-01 to 2027-03-14 | 2027-02-15 to 2027-03-14 |
| Emergency roster registration deadline | 2027-02-06 | 2027-02-12 |

Stage 1 is compressed rather than extended. First Stand 2027 is unchanged
(2027-03-20 to 2027-03-28, Berlin, Germany). Stage 2, Stage 3, the Championship
Stage, the July global break, and all Counter-Strike dates are unchanged.
Official Tier 2 leagues may adjust schedules, but no new Tier 2 dates are
confirmed and existing values are preserved.

## Explicitly not established

- No external player's nationality, region, team, or contract is rewritten.
- No named player retirement, transfer, or 2027 external roster is established;
  Episode 111 describes Korean retirements only in general terms.
- No First Stand qualifiers, groups, brackets, or results.
- No Tier 2 start dates.
