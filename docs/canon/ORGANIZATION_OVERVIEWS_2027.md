# Organization Overviews, 2027

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED
**Source type:** Real-world reference (Wikipedia introductions and public esports reporting, retrieved 2026-11-09)

Backs `data/competitive-world-overrides/organization-overviews-2027.json`:
107 organization overviews (68 League of Legends, 50 Counter-Strike, shared organizations counted once)
and 21 Tier 2 team overviews for organizations that have no Tier 1 parent.

Each overview describes only the organization: where it is based, when it was founded,
what it competes in and its main honours. Facts come from the sources above. Where the
sources conflict or give nothing, the overview states less rather than guessing.
Organizations about which little is public (university programmes and small Tier 2
organizations) have a short description of the league they compete in.

Tier 2 and academy teams show the overview of their parent organization.
