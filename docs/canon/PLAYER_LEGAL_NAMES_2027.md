# Leaguepedia Legal Names, 2027 Nickname-Only Players

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED
**Source type:** Real-world reference (Leaguepedia player pages, fetched 2026-11-09)

Backs `data/competitive-world-overrides/player-legal-names-2027.json` (227 legal names)
and the matching nationalities added to `player-nationalities-2027.json`.

Each nickname-only identity was looked up on Leaguepedia. A name is recorded
only when the nickname resolved to one player whose residency matches the
player's league and whose role is a playing role.

- Korean and Chinese leagues keep the league nationality directed earlier.
- Other leagues take the player's Leaguepedia country as the nationality.
- Names the page lists as retired, in another region, or as a non-player role are not used.

Popp, Xiang, Solid, fragio and Nordik (Mathieu Gagnon, French-speaking Canada) have no usable Leaguepedia record; their names were created at the author's request.
Prime, Cracker, Guardian, lime, Unforgiven, Liang, Adiza1, Roger and Sniper1 were matched to the Leaguepedia pages the author named.
