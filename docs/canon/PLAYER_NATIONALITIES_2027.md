# Author Directive: Roster Table Regions and Nationalities

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED
**Source type:** Direct author-confirmed canon directive (not sourced from episode prose)

Backs `data/competitive-world-overrides/player-nationalities-2027.json`.

## Region column

The Region column of every external roster table shows the league's region
code, not a value derived from the player:

| Leagues | Region |
| --- | --- |
| LCK, LCK Challengers | KR |
| LPL, LDL | CH |
| CBLOL, Circuito Desafiante | SA |
| LCP, LCP Challengers | PCS |
| LEC, EMEA Masters | EU |
| LCS, NACL | NA |

Kairos teams keep the region derived from their players' nationalities.

## NAT column

The NAT column shows the flag of the player's real-world nationality.

- Every player in LCK, LCK Challengers, LPL and LDL is South Korean or Chinese
  respectively, as directed by the author.
- CBLOL and Circuito Desafiante use each player's real nationality, because
  several teams field Spanish-speaking players (Argentina, Chile, Peru,
  Colombia and Venezuela).
- LEC and LCP use each player's real nationality (the Leaguepedia 2026-08-03
  snapshot where it has one, the flags in the author's screenshots, otherwise
  public esports databases).

The overlay holds 104 individual nationalities. Players without an entry have no
nationality established; at the time of writing: none. EMEA Masters players are nickname-only author
identities, so no nationality is recorded for them.
