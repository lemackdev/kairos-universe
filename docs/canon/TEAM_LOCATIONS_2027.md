# Team Locations, 2027

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED
**Source type:** Real-world reference

Backs `data/competitive-world-overrides/team-locations-2027.json`.

- **Org Location:** the country of the organization, shown with its flag.
- **Team Location:** the city where the team is based. Where no city is established it is the
  capital of the country, as directed by the author.
- League of Legends countries come from the Leaguepedia 2026-08-03 snapshot (team `Location` and
  `TeamLocation`); a few organizations missing there were filled from their parent or league.
- Tier 2 and academy teams use their parent organization's locations.
- Counter-Strike org countries come from public reporting (see the organization overviews), team countries and head coaches from HLTV team pages.
