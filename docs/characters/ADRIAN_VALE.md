- Reserved.
- Long-term thinker.
- Highly analytical.
- More interested in building institutions than receiving attention.
- Protective of young players and their futures.

## Philosophy

1. Develop people before pursuing championships.
2. Do not trap players through abusive contracts.
3. Build systems intended to survive for decades.
4. Kairos exists to strengthen the entire North American ecosystem.
