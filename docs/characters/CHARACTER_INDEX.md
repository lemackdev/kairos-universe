## First-Appearance Rule

Episode 1 records Adrian's first appearance through current canon metadata. The
historical prose remains unchanged and does not name its first-person narrator.

Arthur first appears unnamed in Episode 36 and is identified by name in Episode
37. Richard first appears in Episode 54.
