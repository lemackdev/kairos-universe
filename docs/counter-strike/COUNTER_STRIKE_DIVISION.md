## Development System

The Academy is a developmental pathway into the main team. It is not a
decorative roster. Recruitment prioritizes young players with developmental
upside from the United States, Canada, and Mexico under the rules documented in
[Recruitment Rules](RECRUITMENT_RULES.md).

The confirmed roster-building order is:

1. IGL, fulfilled by Caleb "Atlas" Mercer
2. Primary AWPer
3. Star Rifler
4. Entry
5. Anchor / Support

The order has been fulfilled by the completed main roster. Academy players do
not inherit these positions. Their permanent responsibilities remain **TBD**
until Stewie2K observes their development.

## Recruitment Philosophy

- Teams are built before lineups.
- Leadership is more valuable than individual statistics.
- Character is evaluated before contracts.
- Communication under pressure is considered a measurable skill.
- Kairos recruits long-term teammates instead of short-term stars.
- Chemistry is evaluated before individual skill.
- Internal scrims are part of recruitment.
- Former teammates, coaches, and tournament organizers provide character
  references alongside gameplay evidence.
