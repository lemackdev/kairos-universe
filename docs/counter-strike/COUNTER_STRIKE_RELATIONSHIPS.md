# Counter-Strike Relationships

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED at the stated level

## Coaching Relationships

- Lukas "Luk" Novak is Head Coach of
  [Kairos Counter-Strike](MAIN_TEAM.md).
- Jake "Stewie2K" Yip is Head Coach of
  [Kairos Counter-Strike Academy](ACADEMY_TEAM.md) from 23/11/2026.
- Ethan "Trace" Miller is Development Coach from 23/11/2026.
- Trace is Luk's pupil.
- Luk mentors Trace.
- Luk is responsible for the global Kairos Counter-Strike philosophy.
- Stewie2K observes the Academy before assigning player responsibilities.
- Trace supports development methodology, individual development,
  Academy-main integration, the coaching structure, and development of players
  and coaches.

## Team Relationships

- Kairos Counter-Strike and Kairos Counter-Strike Academy belong to the same
  development system.
- The Academy is a developmental pathway into the main team.
- There is no third Counter-Strike roster.
- Shared analysts and performance resources support both teams.
- Shared sports psychology resources exist at a high level.
- The main-team structure is strategically leaner than the League division.

## Organizational Relationships

- Both teams belong to Kairos Esports.
- Kairos Esports is a subsidiary of Asterion Intelligence.
- Adrian Vale, the main protagonist and first-person narrator, founded and owns
  Kairos Esports.
- Adrian oversees every current Counter-Strike recruitment and establishes
  that Kairos recruits people before players.
