# Counter-Strike Relationships

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED at the stated level

## Coaching Relationships

- Lukas "Luk" Novak is Head Coach of
  [Kairos Counter-Strike](MAIN_TEAM.md).
- Ethan "Trace" Miller is Head Coach of
  [Kairos Counter-Strike Academy](ACADEMY_TEAM.md).
- Trace is Luk's pupil.
- Luk mentors Trace.
- Luk is responsible for the global Kairos Counter-Strike philosophy.
- Trace translates Luk's philosophy into academy training while developing his
  own coaching identity.

## Team Relationships

- Kairos Counter-Strike and Kairos Counter-Strike Academy belong to the same
  development system.
- The Academy is a developmental pathway into the main team.
- There is no third Counter-Strike roster.
- Shared analysts and performance resources support both teams.
- Shared sports psychology resources exist at a high level.
- The main-team structure is strategically leaner than the League division.

## Organizational Relationships

- Both teams belong to Kairos Esports.
- Kairos Esports is a subsidiary of Asterion Intelligence.
- Adrian Vale, the main protagonist and first-person narrator, founded and owns
  Kairos Esports.
- Adrian oversees the early construction of the Counter-Strike division.

## Player Relationships

- Adrian Vale is Caleb "Atlas" Mercer's founder, mentor, and evaluator. Their
  mutual respect is confirmed.
- Luk is Caleb's Head Coach and tactical evaluator.
- Trace is Caleb's tactical mentor.
- Caleb and Owen "Glacier" Fletcher are prospective teammates. Their first
  meeting is complete, and their mutual respect is confirmed.
- Caleb has an initial-evaluation relationship with Cameron "Mold" Ruiz.
- Caleb has tactical-evaluation interest in Emiliano "MoteConHuesillo" Navarro.
- Caleb is the Founding Captain and only confirmed Counter-Strike captain.

Exact friendships, conflicts, rivalries, and personal bonds are **TBD**.
