# IGL Scouting

**Document type:** Confirmed process and outcome

**Current completion status:** First-IGL objective fulfilled

Caleb "Atlas" Mercer is the first Counter-Strike player signed by Kairos, the
IGL, and the Founding Captain. This document preserves the evaluation framework
without inventing stage-by-stage results that are not present in the approved
source.

## Evaluation Objective

Kairos intends to find a young, developing leader who can become the first
structural piece of the roster. The candidate need not be a high-fragging
player or a finished veteran leader.

The process should evaluate elite map understanding, calling, mid-round
adaptation, leadership potential, communication, emotional control, pattern
recognition, responsibility, teammate development, and coordination of
different personalities.

## Planned Workflow

### 1. Regional Discovery

Identify eligible prospects from the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Check
the confirmed age policy and, for Mexican candidates, English communication
fluency.

Discovery alone does not create a contract or roster position.

### 2. Demo Review

Evaluate map understanding, calling patterns, positioning, adaptation, utility
decisions, risk selection, and recurring mistakes.

### 3. Communication Assessment

Evaluate clarity, timing, relevance, emotional control, listening, and the
ability to coordinate different personalities. Communication quality matters
more than volume.

### 4. Tactical Interview

Evaluate how the candidate explains decisions, recognizes patterns, accepts
responsibility, and responds when the initial plan fails.

### 5. Trial Environment

Place a candidate in a controlled team setting to observe live calling,
adaptation, communication, and response to coaching. Caleb completed multiple
tactical evaluations, but the exact trial format and results are **TBD**.

### 6. Team-Building Simulation

Evaluate how the candidate would fit with different role profiles and how the
candidate approaches complementary roster construction.

### 7. Emotional And Leadership Assessment

Evaluate composure, accountability, conflict response, resilience, and the
ability to support teammates through difficult rounds.

### 8. Development Projection

Estimate how the candidate's decision-making, leadership, mechanics, and
communication could develop within the Kairos system.

### 9. Final Recommendation

Staff should consolidate evidence, risks, development needs, and roster fit.
A recommendation is not a signing.

### 10. Contract Discussion

Contract discussion may begin only after a final recommendation. Caleb's signed
status is confirmed. His exact signing date, contract duration, salary, and
negotiation details are **TBD**.
