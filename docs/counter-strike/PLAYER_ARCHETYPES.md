# Counter-Strike Player Archetypes

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED as recruitment guidance

These archetypes define what Kairos intends to evaluate. They are not player
records, candidates, promises of skill level, or completed scouting results.

## IGL

**Recruitment order:** 1

**Initial scouting weight:** 30%

Style references:

- karrigan
- apEX

These are style references only. A future Kairos IGL must not be described as
equal to either player in skill, personality, career, or achievements.

Primary qualities:

- Elite map understanding
- Strong calling
- Mid-round adaptation
- Leadership potential
- Communication
- Emotional control
- Ability to improve teammates
- Ability to recognize patterns
- Willingness to accept responsibility
- Ability to coordinate different personalities

The IGL does not need to be a high-fragging player. Leadership may still be
developing because the candidate will be young. The future IGL must not be a
finished veteran leader.

## Primary AWPer

**Recruitment order:** 2

**Initial scouting weight:** 25%

Style references:

- ZywOo
- molodoy

These are style references only. The future AWPer must not be defined as a copy
of either player.

Primary qualities:

- Precision
- Consistency
- Positioning
- Calmness
- Discipline
- Low number of wasted shots
- Ability to remain useful without forced aggression
- Reliability under pressure

## Star Rifler

**Recruitment order:** 3

**Initial scouting weight:** 20%

Style references:

- NiKo
- donk

These are style references only. They do not establish equivalent mechanics,
personality, career, or achievements.

Primary qualities:

- Elite mechanics
- Frequent headshots
- Movement
- Spray control
- Confidence
- Ability to decide rounds individually
- High mechanical ceiling
- Willingness to take responsibility in important rounds

The player may still need tactical development.

## Entry

**Recruitment order:** 4

**Initial scouting weight:** 15%

Primary qualities:

- Creates space
- Initiates engagements
- Enables star players
- Understands timing
- Accepts high-risk responsibilities
- Maintains confidence after difficult rounds
- Communicates useful information immediately
- Does not chase statistics at the expense of structure

## Anchor / Support

**Recruitment order:** 5

**Initial scouting weight:** 10%

Primary qualities:

- Defensive discipline
- Utility usage
- Positional patience
- Late-round decision-making
- Reliability
- Team-first mentality
- Ability to stabilize difficult rounds
- Willingness to perform low-visibility responsibilities
- Communication under pressure

## Interpretation Rules

- Scouting weights represent recruitment priority, not in-game importance.
- Reference profiles provide direction only.
- No future player is guaranteed to match every listed quality.
- No candidate or roster member exists yet.
- Exact candidate evaluations and development projections are **TBD**.
