# Counter-Strike Recruitment Rules

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED unless explicitly labeled otherwise

## Eligible Recruitment Region

Kairos may recruit Counter-Strike players from:

- United States
- Canada
- Mexico

Mexican candidates must communicate fluently in English. This is a recruitment
condition, not a statement that every Mexican player automatically qualifies.

No additional country restriction or eligibility rule is confirmed.

## Age Policy

The completed Academy selection establishes these active ages:

- 15
- 16

The protected 0.7.0 rule stating 16 through 18 for the Academy is superseded by
Episodes 63 through 74. The completed Academy roster contains players aged 15
or 16. All are American minors living in California. Exact dates of birth
remain **TBD** and cannot be derived from age.

The main-roster age record is separate. Cameron "Mold" Ruiz is 18, not 19.

## Recruitment Order

1. IGL
2. Primary AWPer
3. Star Rifler
4. Entry
5. Anchor / Support

The first signed player had to be the IGL. Caleb "Atlas" Mercer fulfilled this
requirement as the first signed player and Founding Captain. The remaining
order defines roster-construction priority and does not determine in-game
importance.

## Academy Scouting Boundary

- Main-team players do not participate in Academy scouting or recruitment.
- Main-team players remain focused on training and competition preparation.
- Academy scouting is handled by management, scouting staff, analysts, and
  canonically confirmed Academy coaching staff.
- Stewie2K discussion is exploratory before Episode 74.
- Stewie2K becomes active Academy Head Coach on 23/11/2026.
- Trace becomes active Development Coach on 23/11/2026 and remains part of
  Kairos.
- No permanent Academy position, captain, IGL, substitute, or starter
  hierarchy is assigned.

## Evaluation Rules

- Teams are built before lineups.
- Character is evaluated before contracts.
- Communication under pressure is measurable.
- Leadership is evaluated outside official matches.
- Internal scrims may be used for recruitment evaluation.
- Former teammates, coaches, and tournament organizers may provide character
  references.
- Kairos recruits people and long-term teammates, not short-term stars.

## Nickname Policy

Future Counter-Strike nicknames should feel natural, personal, strange,
abbreviated, accidental, or culturally specific.

The general naming feeling may be informed by names such as donk, m0NESY, jL,
ZywOo, HeavyGod, and kyxsan. These names must not be copied or imitated
directly.

Avoid generic heroic or cinematic nicknames such as:

- North
- Shadow
- Phoenix
- Reaper
- Titan
- Phantom

This is a creative guideline, not a technical validation restriction. The
project must not create a fixed nickname generator.

## Connor "North" Sullivan

Connor "North" Sullivan was a removed, non-canonical concept for a 24-year-old
IGL candidate.

- He cannot appear in the current candidate pool.
- He cannot appear as a signed Kairos player.
- He may appear only in the immutable historical archive, retcon
  documentation, or explicit non-canonical context.
- The nickname "North" cannot be reused for another player.
- He is not Connor "Mako" Sullivan from the League of Legends division.

The two names refer to separate records and concepts. Connor "Mako" Sullivan
remains untouched.

See [Retcon History](../retcons/RETCON_HISTORY.md) for the authoritative retcon
entry.

The historical Episode 26 interview remains preserved in the archive but does
not establish a current evaluation, candidate, or player.
