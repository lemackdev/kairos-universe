# Counter-Strike Scouting Philosophy

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED

## Core Principle

Kairos is building a long-term competitive and developmental system. It is not
buying an established roster. Scouting must identify young players whose
decision-making, communication, role fit, and growth potential can be developed
within that system.

## Luk's Coaching Principles

1. Systems should develop players rather than hide their weaknesses.
2. Young players should learn decision-making, not merely execute fixed calls.
3. Structure and creativity must coexist.
4. Communication quality matters more than volume.
5. A roster should be built around complementary roles.
6. The IGL is the first structural piece.
7. Long-term player growth matters more than early rankings.

## Recruitment Priority

| Order | Role | Initial scouting weight |
| ---: | --- | ---: |
| 1 | IGL | 30% |
| 2 | Primary AWPer | 25% |
| 3 | Star Rifler | 20% |
| 4 | Entry | 15% |
| 5 | Anchor / Support | 10% |

The weights measure recruitment priority. They do not rank the roles' in-game
importance.

The first signed player had to be the IGL. Caleb "Atlas" Mercer fulfilled that
requirement and is the Founding Captain. Owen "Glacier" Fletcher, Cameron
"Mold" Ruiz, and Emiliano "MoteConHuesillo" Navarro are canonical unsigned
prospects.

## Evaluation Focus

Scouts should evaluate:

- Developmental upside
- Decision-making
- Communication quality
- Emotional control
- Role compatibility
- Ability to improve within a structured system
- Ability to complement the eventual roster
- Responsibility under pressure

Role-specific qualities are documented in
[Player Archetypes](PLAYER_ARCHETYPES.md). The planned first-IGL process is
documented in [IGL Scouting](IGL_SCOUTING.md).
