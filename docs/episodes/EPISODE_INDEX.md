# Episode Index

All historical sources for Episodes 1 through 29 are recovered. Episodes 30
through 35 have approved titles and structured metadata, but their source prose
has not been supplied. `TBD` in the current-title column means no current-canon
English title has been approved; it does not mean the historical title is
missing.

## Narrator and Protagonist

Current canon metadata identifies Adrian Elias Vale, commonly known as Adrian
Vale, as the narrator and protagonist of Episodes 1 through 35. Episode 1,
"O Começo," records his first appearance.

The historical prose remains unchanged and leaves the first-person narrator
unnamed. Adrian's identity is metadata and must not be inserted retroactively
into archived dialogue or narration.

## Status

- Episodes 1 through 29 are `reconstructed` and `source-recovered`.
- Episodes 30 through 35 are `partially-reconstructed` and
  `source-recovery-required`.
- Episodes 1 through 29 cite the archived PDF. Episodes 30 through 35 cite the
  approved canon batch document.
- Current titles for Episodes 1 through 24 remain **TBD**.
- Every episode record uses the confirmed story year 2026. Exact calendar days
  remain **TBD**.
- Every existing episode record resolves `narrator` and `protagonist` to
  `adrian-elias-vale`.
