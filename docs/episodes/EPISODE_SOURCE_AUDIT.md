## Confirmed Historical Findings

- Episode 1 uses Northstar Esports before later episodes use Kairos Esports.
- Episodes 2, 5, and 9 preserve the historical League aliases later replaced
  by rkN, Vanta, 404, Aster, and Moss.
- Episode 4 contains the incorrect identity David Turley for Aphromoo.
- Episodes 15-21 contain the Korean bootcamp.
- Episodes 19-20 contain the T1 Academy recording inconsistency.
- Episode 22 returns the League division to Los Angeles and explicitly states
  the roles of Kite, Vex, and June.
- Episodes 23-24 begin the Counter-Strike project and scouting.
- Episode 25 introduces Luk and Trace.
- Episode 26 contains the removed Connor "North" Sullivan concept.
- Episode 27 establishes the late October 2026 phase.
- Episode 28 is duplicated and contains the coaching-timeline inconsistency.
- Episode 29 defines the first-IGL profile without selecting a player.

## Current-Canon Corrections Applied

- Kairos Esports replaces Northstar Esports.
- Current League nicknames replace the historical aliases.
- Zaqueri "Aphromoo" Black replaces the incorrect historical identity.
- The story year is confirmed as 2026. Exact calendar days remain **TBD** unless
  separately established.
- The normalized timeline follows the current canonical sequence.
- Connor "North" Sullivan is excluded from active character arrays and player
  records.
- Episode 28 has one structured record.
