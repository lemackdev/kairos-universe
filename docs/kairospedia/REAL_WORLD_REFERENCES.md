# Real-World References

Real-world people and organizations may appear as references inside the
fictional Kairos continuity.

## Required distinction

- `originType: fictional` identifies an entity created for the Kairos
  Universe.
- `originType: real-world-reference` identifies a real-world entity referenced
  by the fictional continuity.
- Every fictional Kairos event remains a Kairos Universe event, even when a
  real-world reference participates.

## Public notice

Every Kairospedia page states that fictional matches and events involving
real-world organizations are not real-world results.

## Source rules

- Real-world background requires an appropriate source.
- Fictional Kairos involvement requires repository canon.
- The two claims must not share an ambiguous label.
- A real-world reference does not authorize scraping or importing third-party
  data.
- Real-world branding and proprietary media are not copied.

## Current release

Aphromoo, Thinkcard, Darshan, Xmithie, Pobelter, and Doublelift are real-world
esports figures appearing in fictional Kairos roles.

Only Aphromoo and Thinkcard have staff pages in the current publication.
Darshan, Xmithie, Pobelter, Doublelift, and Aphromoo appear in confirmed
mentorship relationships without implying that every mentor is full-time team
staff.
