## Current state

- Academy White is active and training in Los Angeles.
- Academy White is a U19 development roster.
- Academy White has not played an official match.
- Official competitive debut is planned for February 2027.
- The League division previously completed a Korean bootcamp and returned to
  Los Angeles.

U19 development is the roster's confirmed purpose. The confirmed player ages
range from 16 through 17. The purpose does not establish an official
competition entry.

## Development purpose

Academy White belongs to the same development system as the main team and
Academy Blue. It is the younger development roster and exists for genuine
player progression.
