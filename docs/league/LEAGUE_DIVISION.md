## Current competitive state

- All three teams are active and training in Los Angeles.
- None of the teams has played an official match.
- The main team has not debuted in the LCS.
- Official competitive debut is planned for February 2027.
- February 2027 is a planned period, not a completed event.
- The League division previously completed a Korean bootcamp.
- The League division has returned to Los Angeles.
- Competitive history, achievements, and statistics are currently empty.

## Main competitive project

Zaqueri "Aphromoo" Black is the Head Coach responsible for the main League
competitive project. Thinkcard is the Strategic Coach, with a confirmed focus
on strategy and competitive structure. Emily Chen supports Analytics and
Performance.

Daniel "Moss" Reyes is captain of the main roster. Moss is the only confirmed
captain in the League division.

## Development structure

The division follows the broader Kairos philosophy:

- Player development over superteam construction.
- Long-term infrastructure over short-term publicity.
- Coaching and analytics as core competitive advantages.
- Academy systems with genuine developmental purpose.
- Structured support for young players.
- High competitive ambition without exploiting players.

## Canon constraints

- Do not create match results before an official match is confirmed.
- Do not treat intended LCS or NACL destinations as completed participation.
- Do not invent player nationalities from their cities.
- Do not derive birth dates from current ages.
- Do not infer academy roles from roster order.
- Do not invent contracts, salaries, rankings, or statistics.
- Real-world esports figures in Kairos roles must be labeled as fictional
  involvement.
