## Confirmed structure

- Each main-roster role has a corresponding current mentor.
- Darshan is the Top mentor.
- Xmithie is the Jungle mentor.
- Goldenglue is the current Mid mentor, effective 2027-01-04, succeeding Pobelter.
- WildTurtle is the current ADC mentor, effective 2027-01-04, succeeding Doublelift.
- Aphromoo is the Support mentor.
- Aphromoo is both Head Coach and Support mentor; this Head Coach role is
  unaffected by the Mid/ADC mentor succession and must not be reassigned.

## Real-world figure policy

All mentors (current and former) are real-world esports figures appearing in
fictional Kairos roles. The mentor assignments are fictional canon and must
be distinguished from real-world background.

Do not invent:

- Contract values or salaries.
- Exact contract or start dates.
- Detailed duties beyond the confirmed role assignment.
- Unconfirmed personal relationships with players.
- Real-world career facts not established by this project's canon.
- A reason for the Pobelter/Doublelift mentorship conclusion beyond their own
  confirmed increased content-creation and organizational commitments (no
  conflict, dispute, or disciplinary issue is canon).
