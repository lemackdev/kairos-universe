# League Relationships

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED relationships only

This document records structural and role-based relationships. It does not
infer friendships, rivalries, conflicts, or family-like bonds.

## Team system

- Kairos League of Legends Main Team, Academy Blue, and Academy White belong to
  the same Kairos development system.
- The main team is intended for the LCS.
- Academy Blue is intended for the NACL.
- Academy White is the U19 development roster.
- Both academy teams exist for genuine player progression.
- Academy teams are not decorative rosters.

## Leadership

- Adrian Vale founded and owns Kairos Esports.
- Adrian personally participates in recruiting and developing the original
  League of Legends program.
- Daniel "Moss" Reyes is captain of the main team.
- Moss is the only confirmed League captain.
- Zaqueri "Aphromoo" Black is Head Coach of the League division.
- Aphromoo is responsible for the main League competitive project.
- Aphromoo is also the Support mentor.
- Aphromoo has expanded mentorship of Academy players.
- Communication and team culture are measurable development goals.
