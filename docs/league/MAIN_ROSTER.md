## Leadership

Daniel "Moss" Reyes is captain of the main roster. No other League player is a
confirmed captain.

Zaqueri "Aphromoo" Black is the Head Coach responsible for the main League
competitive project. Aphromoo also serves as Moss's Support mentor.

During Phase Two, Moss is responsible for helping the roster discover its
competitive identity when the initial plan fails. This does not alter any
player role or captaincy.
