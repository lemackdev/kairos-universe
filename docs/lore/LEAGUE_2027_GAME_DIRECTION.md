# League of Legends Game Direction in the Kairos Universe

Episode 87 establishes a **fictional Kairos Universe Riot policy**, not a claim
about real-world Riot Games. After 2027, League of Legends will stop releasing
new champions. Already-in-production final champions may release during 2027;
their identities and release dates are TBD.

The game does **not** enter maintenance mode. Development instead deepens the
existing game through champion reworks, visual and gameplay updates, map
evolution, items, objectives, runes, systems, seasonal mechanics, game modes,
and competitive balance.

The 2027 international ecosystem includes First Stand, Crossroads Cup,
Mid-Season Invitational, Rift Masters, World Championship, and Demacia Cup
Global at Tier 1; Crossroads Challengers, Rift Masters Challengers, and WSCI
at Tier 2. Worlds remains highest prestige, while WSCI is the most important
global Tier 2 event. Global Circuit Points connect regional and international
performance, but exact point tables are TBD.

See the [competition records](../../index/competitions.json) for confirmed
allocation and format details. No Kairos team has qualified automatically;
no match, standings, trophy, or official appearance is implied.
