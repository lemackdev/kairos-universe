# Kairos Campus

**Canon status:** CONFIRMED at the conceptual level

## Location

The Kairos headquarters and main campus are located in Los Angeles, California,
United States.

## Purpose

The campus is intended to support:

- Training
- Coaching
- Analytics
- Health
- Performance
- Content
- Administration

It supports the organization's long-term player development model and provides
shared resources where appropriate.

## Confirmed elements

- A separate League of Legends building.
- A separate Counter-Strike building.
- Shared operational areas.
- Shared performance resources where appropriate.
- Space reserved for future esports divisions.

## Project Casa Arthur

Project Casa Arthur was introduced as a conditional plan in Episode 54. By
Episode 86, Arthur Fletcher has relocated to the campus residential area.
The exact move date, medical details, and whether a separate Project Casa
Arthur facility was built remain TBD.

- Intended resident: Arthur Fletcher.
- Required condition: safe travel.
- Required condition: Arthur must receive independent medical approval.
- Planned location: within the Kairos campus property.
- Planned layout: one bedroom, living room, kitchen, and garden.
- Planned functions: full accessibility and proximity to campus medical
  resources.
- Intended purpose: allow Owen to balance career and family.

## Recreation Center

The Recreation Center is operating by Episode 86 as a space for player rest
and wellbeing, not as a public competition arena. Its dimensions, cost, and
exact opening date remain TBD.

## Food-Service Policy

On 07/11/2026, the 143-person Kairos community approved a restricted
food-service proposal by 126 votes to 17. In-N-Out Burger, Chipotle Mexican
Grill, and
Chick-fil-A were selected for invitations and negotiations only. No restaurant
partnership, contract, campus opening, or construction is confirmed.

The policy allows no more than three restaurant chains.

Each person may use each restaurant once per week, with a maximum of three
fast-food meals per week. Purchases are enforced through the campus badge. The
same limits and Kairos nutritional standards apply to players, coaches, staff,
and Adrian. Alcoholic beverages are prohibited.
