Kairos Esports was created as a long-term talent development project. It is
designed to develop players rather than immediately purchase established
superstars. The organization accepts that developed players may eventually
leave for larger opportunities.

Adrian Vale founded and owns Kairos Esports. He personally participates in the
recruitment and development of the original League of Legends program and
oversees the early construction of the Counter-Strike division.

Historical Episode 1 uses the development name Northstar Esports. Current
canon uses Kairos Esports. See the
[Canon Inconsistency Audit](../retcons/CANON_INCONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md).

## Organizational philosophy

1. Player development over superteam construction.
2. Long-term infrastructure over short-term publicity.
3. Coaching and analytics are core competitive advantages.
4. Academy systems must have real developmental purpose.
5. Young players should receive structured support.
6. The organization should avoid exploiting players.
7. Competitive ambition remains extremely high.
8. Winning Worlds and Counter-Strike Majors are long-term goals.
9. Kairos is being built for a multi-year narrative.
10. Canon consistency is more important than improvisational convenience.

Long-term sustainability matters more than immediate trophies.

## League of Legends division

- The division has a main team, Academy Blue, and Academy White.
- The main team is intended for the LCS.
- Academy Blue is intended for the NACL.
- Academy White is a younger development roster.
- All three teams are training.
- No official debut has occurred.
- Official League and NACL competition is scheduled to begin in February 2027.

## Counter-Strike division

- The division has a main team and an academy team.
- There is no third Counter-Strike team.
- Coaching staff has been hired.
- Lukas "Luk" Novak is Head Coach of Kairos Counter-Strike.
- Jake "Stewie2K" Yip is Head Coach of Kairos Counter-Strike Academy from
  23/11/2026.
- Ethan "Trace" Miller is Development Coach from 23/11/2026 and remains active.
- Caleb "Atlas" Mercer is the first signed Counter-Strike player.
- Caleb is the IGL / Rifler, Founding Captain, and only confirmed
  Counter-Strike captain.
- Caleb participates in future roster construction.
- Owen "Glacier" Fletcher is signed as the Primary AWPer.
- Emiliano "MoteConHuesillo" Navarro is signed as a Rifler.
- Cameron "Mold" Ruiz is signed as Entry from 16/11/2026.
- Vincente "FLACOGOMEZ" Galán is signed as Support / Anchor from 16/11/2026.
- Caleb, Owen, and Emiliano participated in the first internal Counter-Strike
  scrim.
- Kairos evaluates communication, trust, character, and cultural fit alongside
  gameplay.
- The five-player main roster is complete.
- The Counter-Strike Academy roster is complete with five selected American
  minors living in California.
- No Academy permanent position, captain, IGL, substitute, or starter hierarchy
  has been assigned.
- The completed main roster plays a fictional-universe private practice series
  against Team Liquid on 20/11/2026. It is not an official match.
- Official Counter-Strike competition is scheduled to begin in February 2027.
