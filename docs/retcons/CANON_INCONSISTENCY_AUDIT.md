# Canon Inconsistency Audit

**Document type:** Historical-to-current canon comparison
**Source priority:** Current repository, this audit, historical PDF

## Purpose

The original episodes are historical source material. They must not be
rewritten or silently modified. When an original episode conflicts with current
canon, the archived source preserves the historical wording while the living
repository reflects the latest approved canon.

The archived source and page map are recorded in the
[Historical Source Manifest](../../archive/SOURCE_MANIFEST.md).

## 1. Organization Name

**Historical version:** Episode 1 introduces the organization as Northstar
Esports. Episode 2 and later episodes use Kairos Esports.

**Current canon:** The organization is Kairos Esports.

**Status:** Resolved retcon.

Northstar Esports is an early development name. It may appear only in
historical source notes and retcon documentation.

## 2. League Main-Roster Nicknames

## 3. Aphromoo Identity

**Historical version:** Episode 4 incorrectly identifies Aphromoo as David
Turley.

**Current canon:** Aphromoo is Zaqueri "Aphromoo" Black.

**Status:** Historical factual error.

The original episode remains unchanged. Every current entity record must use
Zaqueri Black.

## 4. Timeline Drift

Several episodes were written before the chronology was normalized. References
to the campus, Riot invitation, Korean bootcamp, return from Korea, LCS
preparation, and Counter-Strike project do not always agree.

The current canonical order is:

1. Kairos is founded.
2. League of Legends infrastructure is completed.
3. Riot offers the LCS slot.
4. The Korean bootcamp occurs.
5. The League division returns to Los Angeles.
6. The Counter-Strike division begins.
7. February 2027 is the next planned competitive milestone.

The PDF's early 2026 reference now aligns with current canon. The story begins
in early 2026 and reaches 24/11/2026 at the current Episode 75 endpoint.

**Status:** Requires chronological normalization.

The [Master Timeline](../timeline/MASTER_TIMELINE.md) follows the current order.
Historical episodes remain unchanged.

## 5. T1 Academy Scrim Recording

**Historical conflict:** Episode 19 says the T1 Academy scrim has no broadcast
and no recording. Episode 20 later shows the narrator reviewing a replay.

**Current interpretation:** There was no public broadcast or public recording.
Internal replay access remains plausible.

**Status:** Minor continuity inconsistency.

This interpretation does not establish that a public VOD exists.

## 6. Counter-Strike Coaching Timeline

**Historical conflict:** Episodes 25 and 26 establish that Lukas "Luk" Novak and
Ethan "Trace" Miller have joined Kairos. Episode 28 later says coaches still
need to be hired before February 2027.

**Current interpretation:** Episode 28 refers to remaining support staff and
coaching infrastructure, not the two Head Coaches.

**Status:** Minor continuity inconsistency.

## 7. Connor "North" Sullivan

**Historical version:** Episode 26 introduces Connor "North" Sullivan, age 24,
as an IGL candidate.

**Current canon:** Connor "North" Sullivan does not exist in active canon.

**Status:** Resolved retcon.

The name may appear only in the immutable historical episode archive, this
audit, the retcon log, and structured episode retcon notes. It must never appear
as a current candidate, signed player, or active character.

## 8. Episode 28 Duplication

## Additional Historical Boundaries

The PDF includes exact facility details, company history, valuation, dates,
ages, statistics, social metrics, and possible future game divisions that are
not all supported by current canon. These details remain historical evidence
and must not be promoted merely because they appear in the source.

In particular:

- Exact campus dimensions, room counts, costs, address, and architectural
  details remain **TBD** in current canon.
- Asterion's detailed corporate history, valuation, and technical portfolio
  remain **TBD** unless separately confirmed by current repository canon.
- VALORANT and Dota references remain historical plans, not confirmed current
  divisions.
- Historical exact ages and nationalities for staff or real-world figures do
  not override current entity fields.
- Historical social metrics, audience numbers, performance measurements, and
  scrim statistics are not current official statistics.

## Episodes 57-75 Source Conflicts

The supplied Episodes 57-75 DOCX is complete, preserved byte-for-byte, and
normalized separately for current-canon use.

Recorded conflicts:

- Episode 57 labels 08/11/2026 as Saturday. The calendar-correct weekday is
  Sunday.
- Episode 57 identifies Arthur as a campus cook. Arthur Fletcher remains
  Glacier's grandfather and is not Kairos staff; the cook remains unnamed.
- Episodes 57, 59, and 62 regress Glacier and MoteConHuesillo toward evaluation
  or a second signing. The protected 0.7.0 state already establishes them as
  signed by 07/11/2026.
- Episodes 63 and 64 place main-roster players in Academy recruitment. Current
  canon keeps recruitment with authorized staff and scouting personnel.
- Episode 66 places Arthur in a recruitment meeting. Current canon excludes
  him from management and recruiting.
- Stewie2K discussion before Episode 74 remains exploratory. His active
  appointment begins on 23/11/2026.

**Status:** Resolved through documented source normalization.

The exact transformations are recorded in
[Episodes 057-075 Source Normalization](../episodes/EPISODES_057_075_NORMALIZATION.md).
The preserved DOCX remains unchanged.

## Preservation Rules

- Never rewrite the historical episodes.
- Never silently overwrite historical facts.
- Preserve original source files byte-for-byte.
- Apply corrections only in the living canon repository.
- Record every future retcon explicitly.
- Preserve unresolved versions side by side until a canonical decision exists.
- Do not create a third version to resolve a conflict.

The repository is the living canon. The archived PDF is the immutable
historical record.
