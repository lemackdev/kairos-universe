# Retcon History

This file records removed, replaced, and clarified concepts. Retconned material
must not appear as current canon.

## Log format

Each entry records:

- Stable retcon ID.
- Classification.
- Previous concept, if one existed.
- Current canon.
- Reason.
- Effective date when known.

The complete source comparison is maintained in the
[Canon Inconsistency Audit](CANON_INCONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md).

## Atlas identity correction

**Retcon ID:** `retcon-counter-strike-atlas-identity`
**Classification:** Continuity correction
**Effective date:** TBD

### Previous identity

- The Counter-Strike IGL and Founding Captain was named Mason Brooks.
- His nickname was Atlas.

### Current canon

- The same character is Caleb Mercer.
- His nickname remains Atlas.
- He remains the first signed Kairos Counter-Strike player.
- He remains the IGL, Founding Captain, and only confirmed Counter-Strike
  captain.
- His first appearance remains Episode 30, "A Voz no Servidor."
- His biography, role, relationships, first appearance, and signed status are
  unchanged.

### Reason

- The previous identity conflicted with Mason "404" Brooks, the established
  League of Legends main-roster Mid laner.
- This correction does not create a second character.
