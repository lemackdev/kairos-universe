- League infrastructure precedes the Riot offer, Korea, the Los Angeles return,
  and the Counter-Strike launch.
- Episode publication order remains sequential.
- Private and internal scrims are not official matches.
- Episode 49 is an internal scrim. Episode 51 is a strategic pressure exercise.
- Caleb remains the first signed Counter-Strike player and Founding Captain.
- Caleb, Owen, and Emiliano are signed by the 07/11/2026 state;
  exact signing chronology for Owen and Emiliano is TBD.
- Cameron and FLACOGOMEZ remain unsigned until both sign on 16/11/2026.
- The completed main roster's Team Liquid series is private practice, not an
  official result, achievement, ranking, or real-world historical match.
- The Counter-Strike Academy has five selected players by 23/11/2026, but no
  permanent positions, captain, IGL, substitute, or starter hierarchy.
- Main-roster players do not scout, interview, approve, reject, or select
  Academy candidates.
- Stewie2K discussions are exploratory before his 23/11/2026 appointment.
- Trace remains active in Kairos as Development Coach from 23/11/2026.
- Project Casa Arthur is planned and conditional, not built or occupied.
- Episode 56 creates invitations and negotiations, not restaurant partnerships.
- The 25/11/2026 Thanksgiving gathering remains planned, not completed.
- December League external scrims remain planned with no named opponent.
- The February 2027 competitive milestone remains planned.
- Adrian is metadata-identified as narrator and protagonist through Episode 75.
