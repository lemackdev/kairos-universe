## Chronology Constraints

- The current order places completed League infrastructure before the Riot
  offer, the Riot offer before Korea, Korea before the Los Angeles return, and
  the return before the Counter-Strike launch.
- Episode publication order is preserved, but historical calendar references
  are normalized through current canon.
- Historical references to early 2026 align with the confirmed story start.
- Episode 13's "first bootcamp" is an internal Kairos training block. It is
  distinct from the Korean bootcamp beginning in Episode 15.
- The private Korean scrims are not official matches and do not constitute
  Kairos's competitive debut.
- Episode 19's recording conflict and Episode 28's coaching statement use the
  interpretations documented in the
  [Canon Inconsistency Audit](../retcons/CANON_INCONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md).
- The February 2027 competitive milestone remains planned and has not
  occurred.
- Adrian is metadata-identified as the narrator and protagonist throughout
  Episodes 1 through 35. The historical prose for Episodes 1 through 29 remains
  unchanged, and Episodes 30 through 35 currently have metadata only.
- Caleb's exact signing episode is **TBD** because no approved Episode 36-42
  source files exist in the repository.

## Retcon Boundaries

- Episode 1's Northstar Esports name is historical only. Current canon uses
  Kairos Esports.
- The historical League aliases Rook, North, Cipher, Forge, and Anchor do not
  replace rkN, Vanta, 404, Aster, and Moss.
- Episode 26's Connor "North" Sullivan material remains only in the historical
  archive and retcon documentation.
- The retired Counter-Strike Atlas identity is replaced by Caleb Mercer. The
  nickname Atlas, first appearance, role, relationships, and signed status are
  unchanged.
- Episode 28 has one timeline entry despite the duplicated PDF pages.
