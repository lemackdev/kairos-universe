# Current World State

- **Canon status:** CONFIRMED
- **Story start:** Early 2026
- **Current story period:** Late October 2026

This document is the mandatory current-state snapshot. It must be reviewed and
updated after every future episode.

## Organization

- Adrian Elias Vale, commonly known as Adrian Vale, is the main protagonist and
  first-person narrator.
- Adrian is the founder and CEO of Asterion Intelligence.
- Adrian is the founder and owner of Kairos Esports.
- Kairos Esports has not made its official competitive debut.

## League of Legends

- The main roster, Academy Blue, and Academy White are active and training in
  Los Angeles.
- None of the three teams has played an official match.
- The main roster has not debuted in the LCS.
- The League division previously completed a Korean bootcamp.
- The League division has returned to Los Angeles.
- Daniel "Moss" Reyes is captain of the main roster.
- Zaqueri "Aphromoo" Black is Head Coach and Support mentor.
- Every main-roster role has a confirmed mentor.
- Official League and NACL competition is scheduled to begin in February 2027.

## Counter-Strike

- Official Counter-Strike competition is scheduled to begin in February 2027.
- Lukas "Luk" Novak is Head Coach of Kairos Counter-Strike.
- Ethan "Trace" Miller is Head Coach of Kairos Counter-Strike Academy.
- Trace is Luk's pupil, and Luk defines the overall divisional philosophy.
- Caleb "Atlas" Mercer is the first signed Kairos Counter-Strike player.
- Caleb is the IGL, Founding Captain, and only confirmed Counter-Strike captain.
- The main roster has one signed player. The Academy roster remains empty.
- No official Counter-Strike match has been played.
- The Academy is a developmental pathway into the main team.
- Scouting remains active for the open main-team positions.
- Owen "Glacier" Fletcher, Cameron "Mold" Ruiz, and Emiliano
  "MoteConHuesillo" Navarro are unsigned prospects.
- Current objective: evaluate unsigned prospects and build the remaining roster
  around Caleb.
